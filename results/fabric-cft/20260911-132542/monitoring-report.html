confirmed_tps: Committed transactions per second, using only T3 (observed-committed) timestamps.  ·  fail_rate: (Invalid + Errored + TimedOut) / Submitted for this measurement window.  ·  e2e: End-to-end latency from the load generator's scheduled send time to observed finality (open-loop, so queueing delay can't hide behind a busy generator).  ·  submit: Latency from the adapter's Submit call entering to the platform acknowledging receipt.  ·  commit: Latency from platform acknowledgement to observed commit in a block.  ·  send-gap: How far behind schedule the load generator ran (actual send time minus scheduled send time). A large p99 here means the generator, not the platform, was the bottleneck.
